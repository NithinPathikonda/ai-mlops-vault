## 💡 How To Use This Workflow
1. **Notice a term at work:** e.g., someone mentions *"We need to store this in ChromaDB"* or *"We need a LangChain agent"*.
2. **Add it to this table.**
3. **Ask Antigravity (or research):** *"Explain this to me like I am going to teach it tomorrow."*
4. **Create a `README.md` and a runnable `demo.py`** in the appropriate folder using [`notes/concept-template.md`](./concept-template.md).
5. **Check it off!**
