# [Concept Name]

## 🧒 1. The 5-Year-Old Analogy
- [Everyday visual metaphor: e.g., a phonebook, a restaurant chef, a conveyor belt, a library card catalog.]

- [Include a simple diagram or flow.]

## 🏢 3. Why Companies Use It (Real-World Impact)
- [What problem does it solve that simpler tools failed at?]
- [What happens if you don't use it?]

- [A standalone script with zero fluff, showing the bare minimum to make it work.]
