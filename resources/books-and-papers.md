# 📚 Recommended Resources, Books & Papers

A curated collection of top-tier resources for Artificial Intelligence and MLOps.

---

## 📖 Essential Books
- **Designing Machine Learning Systems** by Chip Huyen *(Definitive guide to production ML)*
- **Machine Learning Design Patterns** by Valliappa Lakshmanan, Sara Robinson, Michael Munn
- **Deep Learning** by Ian Goodfellow, Yoshua Bengio, and Aaron Courville
- **Hands-On Machine Learning with Scikit-Learn, Keras, and TensorFlow** by Aurélien Géron
- **Reliable Machine Learning** by Cathy Chen et al. (O'Reilly)

---

## 📄 Key Research Papers
- **Attention Is All You Need** (Vaswani et al., 2017) – Transformer foundation
- **LoRA: Low-Rank Adaptation of Large Language Models** (Hu et al., 2021) – Efficient fine-tuning
- **Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Knowledge-Intensive NLP Tasks** (Lewis et al., 2020) – RAG foundation
- **Hidden Technical Debt in Machine Learning Systems** (Sculley et al., Google, 2015) – The motivation behind MLOps
